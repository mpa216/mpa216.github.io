01

8-bit deep-learning accelerator, RTL to signed-off GDS

2026 · GF180MCU 180 nm · LibreLane / OpenROAD · pyuvm · cocotb

An INT8 accelerator for a small MNIST GAN generator, taken from RTL to a signed-off macro with open-source EDA only. I co-designed the RTL; the physical implementation, sign-off, the 4-wire serial bridge that wraps the core, and the UVM verification environment are my own work.

One change I'm proud of: replacing the last result-buffer SRAM with a 384-flip-flop register file. Reads became single-cycle, writeback dropped from 48 to 16 cycles, and power fell 9%.

  • 0 violationsDRC, LVS, GDS-XOR and antenna
  • 9 STA cornersclosed at 40 ns (25 MHz)
  • ~84,875 instancessingle-supply 3.3 V macro
  • Bit-exactagainst a Python golden model, through gate-level re-simulation

Status. Paper accepted at APSIPA ASC 2026 (4th of 7 authors, travel grant received). The block is in the Chipathon 2026 shuttle review, with tape-out expected in September or December 2026. It has not been fabricated yet.

02

Closed-loop chemical dosing for a water-treatment plant

2026 · Bachelor's thesis · ESP32 · C/C++ · MQTT · KiCad

The dosing subsystem of ITB Jatinangor's automated water-treatment plant, designed alone. An ESP32 runs a three-channel non-blocking state machine: it takes flow setpoints over MQTT from the plant's MPC controller, estimates the dosed volume from an ultrasonic level sensor, and drives the soda-ash, PAC and hypochlorite pumps.

I also designed the hardware. The two-layer board went through three fabricated revisions. The hand-built power conditioner holds about 30,000 µF in reserve, so the controller can save its state when the mains drops and resume by itself afterwards.

  • 3.2–10.2%deviation from the 30-minute target across five demand profiles
  • 1.7%volume-estimation error, checked with a graduated cylinder
  • 300+ minutesrelay thermal test, plus automatic recovery after power loss
  • 6 of 7specifications met

What's left. The pump-failure alarm tracks cumulative deficit, so it fired in 7.4–106 s against a 10 s target. That is the next thing to fix.

03

5-bit pipelined ADC, full-custom layout in 45 nm CMOS

2025 · Cadence Virtuoso · switched-capacitor MDAC · MIM capacitors

I cascaded 1.5-bit switched-capacitor stages with digital error correction into a 5-bit pipelined ADC, then laid it out by hand. The work included a modular floorplan that isolates digital logic from the op-amps and comparators, an H-rail power grid, and MIM capacitors in place of MOS capacitors for better matching.

Resizing the comparator capacitors from 100 fF to 500 fF stabilised the first stage. Further down the pipeline, the least significant bits still stick in system simulation.

Honest outcome. DRC and LVS did not pass. Unconstrained auto-routing left two opens on the VSS mesh, and N-well tap density fell short. I traced both to their root causes and specified the fix: hand-constrained ground routing, with well taps placed before any signal routing.

04

Real-time vehicle counting from drone and CCTV video

2025 · Research assistant, LSKK Lab · YOLOv11 · ByteTrack · Python

This pipeline detects, tracks and counts cars, motorcycles, buses and trucks for each direction of an intersection. The stock COCO model failed from a top-down drone view, especially on motorcycles. A bigger model would have fallen to 3–7 FPS, so I fixed the data instead: I annotated 526 drone frames myself and fine-tuned on them.

  • ~14 FPSYOLO11s, chosen after benchmarking all five model sizes
  • 526 framesannotated by hand with Roboflow and SAM
  • 0.65–0.75ByteTrack confidence band that gave stable track IDs
  • Recorded footageevaluated against manual counts, not yet deployed live
